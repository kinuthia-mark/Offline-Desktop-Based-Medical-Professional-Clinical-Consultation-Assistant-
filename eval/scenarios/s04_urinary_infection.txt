Patient: For three days it has been burning when I pass urine, and I go very often.

Doctor: Any blood in the urine, or any vaginal discharge?
Patient: No blood and no discharge.
Doctor: When was your last period, and could you be pregnant?

Doctor: We will check to be sure. Do you take any medicines, and do you have any allergies?

Doctor: Thank you, that is important. Your temperature is thirty-six point nine and blood pressure one twenty-four over seventy-eight. The urine dipstick is positive for nitrites and leukocytes. The pregnancy test is negative. Your abdomen is soft, with mild tenderness above the pubic bone.
Patient: Is it an infection?
Doctor: Yes, this looks like a bladder infection. I thought about ciprofloxacin, but I will not give it, because it is not needed for a simple infection. Because of your sulfa allergy, I will also avoid co-trimoxazole. I will give you nitrofurantoin one hundred milligrams twice a day for five days.
Patient: Okay.
Doctor: Drink plenty of water. If you get a fever, back pain, or vomiting, come back the same day. If you are not better in three days, come back to see me.
Patient: Thank you, doctor.
