OFFLINE CLINICAL CONSULTATION ASSISTANT

This program helps a clinician document a consultation. It records the conversation, turns the
speech into text, and drafts a clinical note (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) together
with a short list of possible diagnoses. The clinician reviews and corrects everything, writes
their own assessment, and confirms the patient's history before a record is saved.

Everything runs on this computer. No part of a consultation is sent over the internet.


WHAT WILL BE INSTALLED

  - The application, in Program Files.
  - The speech recognition model (Whisper small) and the language model (MedGemma 4B), copied
    from the "bundle" folder next to this installer. Every file is checked before anything is
    installed.
  - The local model service (Ollama), if it is not already on this computer.
  - Windows Firewall rules that stop the application and the model service from sending
    anything off this computer. They are removed if the program is uninstalled.


REQUIREMENTS

  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  - 8 GB of memory (close other programs during consultations for the best speed)
  - About 5 GB of free disk space
  - A microphone


PLEASE NOTE

This is a research prototype developed as a final-year project at Strathmore University. It is
not a medical device and has not been approved for clinical use by any health regulatory
authority. Everything it drafts must be checked by a qualified clinician, who remains fully
responsible for the record and for every clinical decision.

Patient records are stored encrypted on this computer and are kept if the program is
uninstalled.
